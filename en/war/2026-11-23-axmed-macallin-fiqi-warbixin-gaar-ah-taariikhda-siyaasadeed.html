Ahmed Moallim Fiqi: A Journey Through Diplomacy, Intelligence, Politics and Leading Somalia's Ministries

A Wartabiye TV special report on the political career of Ahmed Moallim Fiqi, from ambassador to Sudan and head of NISA to Minister of Interior, Foreign Affairs and Defence.

This story was published by Wartabiye TV23.11.2026 · 15:35 (EAT)

SPECIAL REPORT | HISTORY, RECORD AND HIS ROLE IN STATE-BUILDING

Ahmed Moallim Fiqi is a Somali politician who has spent many years in Somalia's politics, security, diplomacy and state-building. Over his political career he has held a range of national posts, including ambassador, head of the intelligence agency, member of the Federal Parliament and minister of several ministries that are central to Somalia's statehood.

Fiqi's political history reflects an official who has passed through many parts of the country's administration, from representing Somalia abroad to internal security, relations with the federal member states, international diplomacy and national defence. These varied posts have made him a political figure whose experience spans several key pillars of statehood.

The start of his diplomatic journey: Somalia's Ambassador to Sudan

In 2010 Ahmed Moallim Fiqi served as Somalia's Ambassador to Sudan, at a time when Somalia was going through a difficult phase of rebuilding its state, its security and its diplomacy.

An ambassadorship is one of the key posts governments use to protect their interests, strengthen bilateral relations, develop cooperation and represent the nation in other countries. For Fiqi, the post was a stage in which he gained experience in relations between states, diplomacy and representing Somalia abroad.

While Somalia was working to rebuild its state institutions, its relations with regional and international partners were vital for political, security and economic support. His ambassadorship in Sudan was part of a political journey that later extended into the institutions of the state.

Leading NISA and national security experience: Director of the National Intelligence and Security Agency

In 2011 Ahmed Moallim Fiqi became director of Somalia's National Intelligence and Security Agency (NISA), at a time when the country faced a very difficult security situation, in particular the war against Al-Shabaab and the rebuilding of the security institutions.

NISA plays an important role in gathering security intelligence, preventing threats, investigating acts of insecurity and supporting other government institutions responsible for protecting national security.

While Fiqi led the agency, Somalia was in a phase in which the government was trying to rebuild its security capacity, facing challenges in equipment, training, funding, administrative capacity and the threat of armed groups.

The experience he gained leading the intelligence agency became one of the most important parts of his political history, as he later returned to ministerial posts directly linked to security and national policy.

Member of Parliament and taking part in national politics

Fiqi also took part in Somali politics as a member of the Federal Parliament, which allowed him to join political debates and work on state-building, the country's laws and relations between state institutions.

Serving as an MP is an important stage for politicians, as it involves representing the public, overseeing the work of government and helping to shape the country's general policies.

The experience he had gained in the security institutions and in diplomacy also gave him a foundation for understanding Somali politics, in particular the challenges of state-building, relations between different centres of power and the situation in the country.

Galmudug Minister of Internal Security: Managing security and the federal member states

Fiqi served as Minister of Internal Security of the Galmudug federal member state between 2020 and 2021.

He took up the post at a time when Somalia's federal member states faced challenges in internal security, managing security forces, relations between security institutions and stability in local areas.

A state-level Ministry of Internal Security is important for coordinating security work, protecting the public, preventing acts of insecurity and making the various institutions of the administration work together.

The post broadened Fiqi's experience of managing security at state level, as he moved on from his intelligence background to the political management of internal security.

Minister of Interior, Federal Affairs and Reconciliation: His role in state-building and federal relations

In August 2022 Ahmed Moallim Fiqi became Minister of Interior, Federal Affairs and Reconciliation of the Federal Government of Somalia.

The Ministry of Interior is one of the most important government institutions, as it plays a role in relations between the Federal Government and the federal member states, internal administration, reconciliation, local government and the development of the federal system.

During his time in the post, the ministry faced many issues central to Somalia's state-building, including:

• Strengthening relations between the Federal Government and the federal member states.

• Developing cooperation between the country's administrations.

• Political reconciliation efforts.

• Supporting the establishment and development of local governments.

• Improving administrative procedures and government services.

• Promoting registration systems and modernising the ministry's services.

• Working on political issues related to state-building and internal stability.

The Interior Ministry post was directly tied to Somalia's domestic politics, since relations between the central government and the federal member states are among the biggest issues affecting the federal system.

The experience Fiqi gained in this post became part of his political journey, and he was later moved to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

Minister of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation: Diplomacy, national interests and international relations

On 7 April 2024 Ahmed Moallim Fiqi was appointed Somalia's Minister of Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation.

The Ministry of Foreign Affairs is the main government institution responsible for diplomatic relations, representing Somalia internationally, agreements with other governments, international cooperation and protecting national interests.

The post brought Fiqi onto the stage of international diplomacy, where Somalia works to strengthen relations with countries in the region, governments around the world, international organisations and partner institutions.

During his time in office, key issues in Somalia's diplomacy included:

• Protecting Somalia's sovereignty and territorial integrity.

• Strengthening relations with regional and international partners.

• Developing political, economic and security cooperation.

• Taking part in international diplomatic conferences and forums.

• Defending Somalia's interests in regional affairs.

• Strengthening Somalia's relations with countries that support state-building and security.

While Fiqi led the Foreign Ministry, Somalia worked continuously on regional issues, relations with neighbouring countries, security cooperation and protecting its sovereignty.

Somalia's diplomacy is especially important at this time, as the country is going through a political and security process that affects its regional and international relations.

Fiqi served as Foreign Minister until 27 April 2025, when he was moved to the Ministry of Defence.

Somalia's Minister of Defence: National defence and reform of the military institutions

On 27 April 2025 Ahmed Moallim Fiqi was appointed Somalia's Minister of Defence.

The Ministry of Defence is responsible for national defence policy, managing the defence institutions, building the capacity of the Somali National Army and Somalia's military cooperation with other countries.

When he took over the post, Somalia faced security challenges, chief among them the war against Al-Shabaab, building the national army, improving operational capacity and developing the country's defence system.

The main things expected of the Defence Ministry's leadership included:

• Strengthening the capacity and professionalism of the Somali National Army.

• Improving the management and structure of the Ministry of Defence.

• Stepping up operations against Al-Shabaab.

• Improving the training, equipment and capacity of the forces.

• Strengthening defence cooperation between Somalia and friendly countries.

• Improving management, transparency and accountability.

• Supporting government plans for security and stability in the country.

The defence portfolio was one of the heaviest responsibilities Fiqi has held, as it was directly tied to national defence, the ongoing war and the future of Somalia's armed forces.

How can his political journey be described?

Looking back at the posts Ahmed Moallim Fiqi has held, it is clear that his experience spans several areas central to Somalia's statehood.

• Security and intelligence: Leading NISA and working on national security.

• State-building and federalism: Managing internal affairs, relations with the federal member states and reconciliation.

• Diplomacy: Serving as ambassador and leading the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, as Somalia protects its national interests and international relations.

• National defence: Leading the Ministry of Defence and the country's defence policy.

The combination of these posts sets his record apart from that of an official confined to a single ministry or department, as he has worked in different institutions all tied to politics, security and state-building.

Political legacy and assessing his record

Ahmed Moallim Fiqi's political history has many sides from which it can be viewed, and each post he held came with its own responsibilities, circumstances and challenges.

The posts he held show experience in administration, security, diplomacy and domestic politics. However, assessing any official's record needs to be based on tangible results, verified information, what was actually achieved and the challenges that existed while he was in office.

It is important to distinguish between the posts he held and the results achieved in those posts, so that his political history is presented in a balanced way based on facts and evidence.

Conclusion: A journey across security, diplomacy and national defence

Ahmed Moallim Fiqi is an official who has held a range of posts central to Somalia's statehood, from ambassador and head of the intelligence agency to member of parliament and minister in the Ministries of Interior, Foreign Affairs and Defence.

His political journey reflects the different phases Somalia has gone through: rebuilding state institutions, strengthening security, developing international relations and building the federal system.

His history can be viewed through the posts he held, the experience he gained in different institutions and the results of the work he did.

Ahmed Moallim Fiqi: Ambassador, Intelligence Director, MP, Security Minister, Interior Minister, Foreign Minister and Defence Minister of Somalia, an official who has worked across many parts of Somalia's statehood.
