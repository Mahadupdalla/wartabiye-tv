Mucaaradka Israa'iil oo baaritaan ka dalbaday sheegashooyin ah in xafiiska Netanyahu uu faragaliyay doorashada

Xildhibaanno iyo hoggaamiyeyaal mucaarad ah oo Israa'iil ah ayaa Isniinta dalbaday baaritaan, kadib warbixin telefishin oo sheegtay in xubin ka tirsan shaqaalaha Ra'iisul Wasaare Benjamin Netanyahu ay booliiska siisay macluumaad ka dhan ah muwaadiniin Carab ah si loo saameeyo doorashadii bishii hore.

Waxaa daabacay Wartabiye TVIsniin, 16 Nofembar 2026 · 22:34 (EAT)

Sheegashooyinkan, oo xafiiska Netanyahu uu beeniyay, ayaa lagu soo bandhigay barnaamij wararka telefishinka Israa'iil ah dhammaadkii usbuuca. Khilaafku wuxuu ku saabsan yahay xarigii la baahiyay ee saddex u dhaqdhaqaaqe oo ka tirsan xisbiga Carabta ee Raam, kuwaas oo koonfurta Israa'iil lagu qabtay subaxnimadii doorashada 27 October, iyadoo lagu eedeeyay qorshe codad lacag lagu iibsanayo.

Waxaa la sheegay in hoggaamiyeyaasha mucaaradka ay ku tilmaameen arrintan isku day afgambi, iyagoo sheegay in markasta oo faahfaahin cusub soo baxdo ay caddaanayso in aanay ahayn oo kaliya isku day lagu carqaladeynayo doorashada.

Xaaladda siyaasadeed

Doorashadaas ayuu xisbiga Likud ee Netanyahu xukunka ku waayay, Netanyahu-na wuxuu hadda yahay ra'iisul wasaare ku-meel-gaar ah inta wadahadallada dhismaha xukuumadda cusub socdaan. Taliyihii hore ee ciidamada Gadi Eisenkot ayaa isku dayaya inuu dhiso isbahaysi cusub.

Lama oga weli in xeer ilaalinta ama hay'adaha kale ay furi doonaan baaritaan rasmi ah oo ku saabsan sheegashooyinka.
