Tanzania oo heshay 1.2 bilyan oo doolar oo lagu dhisayo qaybta Tabora–Kigoma ee tareenka SGR

Wartabiye TVSabti, 7 Nofembar 2026 · 11:23 (EAT)

Tanzania ayaa heshay maalgelin dhan 1.2 bilyan oo doolar oo uu soo abaabulay Bangiga Horumarinta Afrika (AfDB), taasoo lagu dhisi doono qaybta 411 kiiloomitir ee Tabora–Kigoma ee jidka tareenka SGR, sida ay werisay The Citizen. Heshiiska oo ay ku jiraan 9 bangi ayaa qayb ka ah xirmo maalgelin oo dhan 2.3 bilyan oo doolar, waxaana ku jira dammaanad 800 milyan oo doolar ah oo AfDB bixisay.
