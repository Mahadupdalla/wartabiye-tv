Ruto oo ugu baaqay Ntutu iyo Tongoyo inay xakameeyaan taageerayaashooda kadib qalalaasihii dabaaldegga Maa

Madaxweynaha Kenya William Ruto ayaa Axaddii, 15 November, ugu baaqay Guddoomiyaha gobolka Narok Patrick Ole Ntutu iyo xildhibaanka Narok West Gabriel Tongoyo inay xakameeyaan taageerayaashooda, kadib qalalaase ka dhacay dabaaldegga Dhaqanka iyo Dalxiiska Maa ee Sabtidii.

Waxaa daabacay Wartabiye TVAxad, 15 Nofembar 2026 · 17:15 (EAT)

Ruto ayaa hadalkan ka sheegay kadib cibaado uu ka qayb galay kaniisadda St. Peter's Catholic Church ee magaalada Narok, sida ay wariyeen Capital FM Kenya iyo idaacadda dowladda ee KBC. Wuxuu muujiyay walaac uu ka qabo khalkhalkii ka dhacay munaasabadda dhaqanka, isagoo xusay in labada siyaasi ay labaduba ka tirsan yihiin xisbigiisa United Democratic Alliance (UDA).

Madaxweynaha ayaa ka codsaday labada hoggaamiye inay dejiyaan xiisadda siyaasadeed ee u dhexeysa, oo aysan u oggolaan taageerayaashooda in kala duwanaanshahooda uu isu beddelo iska horimaad. Warbixinnada la heli karo kuma faahfaahin in qof ku dhaawacmay qalalaasihii Sabtida.

Waxaa la sheegay in Ruto uu mar kale dhaliilay waxa uu ugu yeeray "siyaasadda hooligaannimada", isagoo sheegay in tartanka doorashada guud ee 2027 uu ahaado mid ku dhisan fikrado, ee aanu ahaan rabshad iyo cabsigelin. Wuxuu ku booriyay musharraxiinta inay ku tartamaan siyaasadahooda iyo waxqabadkooda.

Narok waa gobol ku yaal koonfur-galbeedka Kenya oo ay ku nool yihiin bulshada Maasaayda, dabaaldegga Maa-na waa munaasabad lagu soo bandhigo dhaqanka iyo dalxiiska bulshadaas. Isla booqashadaas, Ruto ayaa sidoo kale ka jawaabay eedaha mucaaradka ee ku saabsan tareenka SGR.

Xiisadda siyaasadeed ee Kenya ayaa sii kordhaysa iyadoo doorashada 2027 ay soo dhowdahay. Hoggaamiyaha mucaaradka Rigathi Gachagua ayaa isna Axaddii sheegay inuu doonayo inuu ka hor Kirismaska heshiis ka gaaro musharrax mideysan oo mucaaradka ah. Lama oga weli tallaabooyin xisbiga UDA uu ka qaadi doono khilaafka Narok.
