Ruushka oo sheegay inuu weeraray saldhigyo koronto iyo 3 markab oo Ukraine ah; boqolaal kun oo aan koronto haysan

Wasaaradda Difaaca Ruushka ayaa sheegtay in weerarrada habeennimo ay ku dhufteen 3 markab oo xamuul ah oo ay ku eedeysay inay hub u sidayeen Ukraine iyo 2 saldhig oo koronto ah, sida ay werisay The Moscow Times. France 24 ayaa sheegtay in boqolaal kun oo Ukrainians ah ay Axaddii koronto la'aan ku noqdeen weerarrada Ruushka ee kaabayaasha tamarta kadib.

Wartabiye TVAxad, 8 Nofembar 2026 · 20:03 (EAT)

Wasaaradda ayaa sheegtay in maraakiibta la weeraray ay ku jireen dekedaha Odesa iyo Chornomorsk ee koonfur-galbeed Ukraine. Sheegashooyinka Ruushka si madax-bannaan looma xaqiijin.
