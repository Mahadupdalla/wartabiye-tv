Wissam Ben Yedder oo ku biiray Paris 13 Atlético ee Ligue 3

Weeraryahankii hore ee AS Monaco iyo xulka Faransiiska, Wissam Ben Yedder oo 36 jir ah, ayaa heshiis la galay kooxda Paris 13 Atlético ee ka ciyaarta Ligue 3, heerka saddexaad ee kubbadda Faransiiska.

Waxaa daabacay Wartabiye TVTalaado, 17 Nofembar 2026 · 15:28 (EAT)

Waxaa la sheegay in Ben Yedder uu u saxiixay kooxda magaalada Paris, halka RMC Sport, oo xigatay bogga Foot Mercato, ay sheegtay in ciyaaryahanku uu heshiis la gaaray kooxda kadib wadahadallo dhawr maalmood socday. Waxaa intaas lagu daray in Paris 13 Atlético ay heshiiskan kaga hor martay kooxda kale ee Ligue 3, Fleury.

Ben Yedder wuxuu dib ugu soo laabanayaa garoomada Faransiiska in ka badan laba sano kadib markii uu ka tagay AS Monaco. Wuxuu hore u ciyaaray Toulouse, wuxuuna xulka Faransiiska u saftay 19 jeer, isagoo u dhaliyay 3 gool.

Paris 13 Atlético ayaa saddexdii xilli ee ugu dambeeyay ka ciyaarayay heerka saddexaad ee Faransiiska, horyaalkaas oo hore loogu yaqaanay National, hadda-na loo yaqaan Ligue 3. Kooxdu waxay hadda ku jirtaa kaalinta 9aad ee horyaalka.

Ciyaaryahan khibrad dheer u leh Ligue 1 iyo kubbadda Yurub ayaa la filayaa inuu xoojiyo weerarka kooxda, inkastoo aan weli la shaacin muddada heshiiska iyo goorta uu ugu horreyn u safan doono.
