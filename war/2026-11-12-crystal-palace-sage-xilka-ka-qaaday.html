Crystal Palace oo xilka ka qaaday tababare Pierre Sage kadib 16 kulan

Kooxda Crystal Palace ayaa Khamiista xilka ka qaaday tababaraha Faransiiska Pierre Sage, iyadoo kooxdu ay ku jirto kaalinta ugu hooseysa Premier League-ka kadib shan bilood oo uu hoggaaminayay.

Waxaa daabacay Wartabiye TVKhamiis, 12 Nofembar 2026 · 15:23 (EAT)

Sage ayaa Palace u tababaray 16 kulan oo dhammaan tartamada ah, wuxuuna ku guuleystay 2 kaliya oo ka mid ah 10-kii kulan ee Premier League-ka. Waxaa la sheegay in kooxda ay is-dhaafsiisay 24 gool horyaalka, taas oo ah tirada ugu badan ee horyaalka. Kulamadiisii ugu dambeeyay waxay ahaayeen guuldarro 2-0 ah oo ay kala kulantay Tottenham iyo guuldarro 3-1 ah oo garoonkeeda Selhurst Park ay kaga timid Liverpool.

Sage waxaa la magacaabay bishii June, isagoo heshiis 3 sano ah la saxiixday, wuxuuna bedelay Oliver Glasner oo kooxda u keenay koobka FA Cup, Conference League iyo Community Shield. Waxaa la sheegay in sidoo kale ay kooxda ka tageen caawiyayaashiisa Jamal Alioui, Damien Della Santa iyo Sandy Guichard. Bayaanka kooxda ayaa sheegay in warbixin ku saabsan magacaabista tababare cusub la bixin doono waqtiga ku haboon.

Waxaa la sheegay in Palace ay ka shaqeyneyso heshiis lala gaaro Thomas Frank, oo shaqo la'aan ahaa tan iyo markii Tottenham ay xilka ka qaadday bishii February. Sage waa tababarihii labaad ee xilliga Premier League-ka xilka laga qaado, kadib markii Manchester United ay isla Khamiista ka saartay Michael Carrick. Kulanka xiga ee Palace waa safar ay ku tageyso Coventry 21 November.
